Frequently Asked Questions

What is Studocu and how does it work?

Studocu is a study platform where students find and share course-specific materials: lecture notes, past exams, summaries, and flashcards from their exact university and course. With over 65 million documents across 180,000+ institutions in 192 countries, you can search by institution, course, or course code and find materials made by students who already passed the class you're preparing for. Studocu AI then turns those materials into mock exams, quizzes, and summaries so you walk into your next exam knowing exactly what to expect.

What is Studocu AI?

Studocu AI is a study tool built on top of Studocu's library of over 65 million student-created materials. Unlike general AI chatbots, it works with your actual course content. Upload your own notes or pull from the Studocu library, and it generates mock exams, quizzes, and summaries tailored to what you're studying. You can also ask it questions about any document and get step-by-step explanations. The result: you prepare for the specific exam in front of you, not a generic version of the subject.

What do I get with Premium?

A big part of the study library on Studocu is accessible without a subscription. Premium unlocks unlimited access to the full library, document downloads, private AI projects, and private class recordings. You can also earn free Premium days by uploading your own study materials.

How do I get free Premium access?

Upload your own original study documents, lecture notes, summaries, or practice materials. Each document goes through a quick review, and once approved, it earns you free Premium days. Documents must be your own work and useful study material.

Is Studocu legit and safe to use?

Yes. Studocu is operated by StudeerSnel B.V., a registered company headquartered in Amsterdam. It's safe to use: payments are processed securely, your personal data is protected under EU privacy law (GDPR), and you control what you upload and share. Studocu also operates under global platform laws, such as the EU's Digital Services Act (DSA) and the US Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA).

Is using Studocu cheating, and is it legal?

Studocu is legal and made for studying, like sharing notes with classmates, just at a global scale. It's not cheating when used the right way: upload only your own original work, never submit someone else's document as yours, don't share copyrighted textbooks, and check your university's sharing rules.

What study materials are on Studocu?

Studocu hosts over 65 million study materials including lecture notes, summaries, past exams, practice questions, and flashcards, uploaded by students at universities, colleges, and schools across 192 countries. Search by institution, course name, or course code to find materials made for exactly your class, often from students who had the same professor. That specificity is what makes the difference when you're preparing for a particular exam, not a general overview of a subject.

How does Studocu prevent spam and low-quality documents?

Every document is checked before it is approved to publish on Studocu, automated checks plus a moderation team filter out spam, duplicates, and copyrighted content. Students then rate documents, so the most helpful ones rise to the top. If you spot a poor or incorrect document, report it on the page and our team will review it.

What AI study tools does Studocu offer?

Studocu AI includes four main tools. The summary tool condenses lecture notes and documents into structured, scannable overviews. The mock exam and quiz generator creates practice questions based on your actual course materials. The Study Assistant answers questions about any document with step-by-step explanations. The class recording feature captures live lectures and automatically produces a summary and quiz from them. Each tool is designed around one outcome: walking into your exam knowing what you'll face and what actually matters.

How does Studocu compare to studying with AI chatbots?

General AI chatbots give you broad answers based on the internet. Studocu AI works from real study materials, either your own uploads or documents from Studocu's library of 65 million student-created resources. That means it can generate a mock exam based on all these resources, or summarize the specific lecture notes from your course. The knowledge comes from students who already took the exam you're preparing for. That's a different kind of preparation.
