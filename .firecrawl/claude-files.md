What it is

## What it is

Upload documents, images and spreadsheets into a chat so Claude can read, summarise or work with them. It’s the first entry in the **+** menu on the left of the [chat composer](https://www.weloveclaude.com/features/chat-composer), and it’s also what drag-and-drop and clipboard paste feed into.

Where to find it

## Where to find it

### On desktop

**Composer → + → Add files or photos**, or press **⌘ U**. The entry is present in the unified **+** menu.

### On the web

Same place on `claude.ai` — **Composer → + → Add files or photos**, ⌘ U — in the unified composer.

How to use it

## How to use it

1. Click **+** on the left of the composer and pick **Add files or photos**, _or_ drag files onto the composer, _or_ paste an image from the clipboard.
2. Choose your files and click **Open**.
3. Send your message. Claude reads the content as part of the turn.

Supported formats

## Supported formats

- **Documents:** PDF, DOCX, CSV, TXT, HTML, ODT, RTF, EPUB, JSON. XLSX works once **code execution and [file creation](https://www.weloveclaude.com/features/file-creation)** are enabled on your account.
- **Images:** JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP.

Limits

## Limits

**Uploading to a chat**

- 500 MB per file.
- Up to 20 files per chat.
- Images up to 8000×8000 pixels.
- PDFs up to 1000 pages. Try a longer one and you’ll get an “Uploaded file is too large” error.

**Uploading to a [project](https://www.weloveclaude.com/features/projects)**

- 30 MB per file.
- No limit on the number of files, but the total has to fit inside Claude’s context window.
- Text extraction only, apart from multimodal PDFs.

Good to know

## Good to know

- **PDFs of 100 pages or fewer** are read for text _and_ visuals — images, charts, graphics. From 101 to 1000 pages Claude processes the text only.
- **Use the page numbers your PDF viewer shows**, not the ones printed on the page, when you point Claude at a specific page.
- **Non-PDF documents are text-only.** An image embedded in a DOCX or a slide deck won’t be read.
- **Large files go further split up.** If you’re near a limit, break the document into sections.
- For what Claude can create back — PDFs, Word files, spreadsheets, slides — see [File creation](https://www.weloveclaude.com/features/file-creation).
