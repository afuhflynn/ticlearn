## What is eStudyUniverse?

Born from MAFA (Mathematical Academia For Africa), we started as a maths-focused community and grew into a full education ecosystem: free PDF downloads on the web, video courses, the MAFA Mathematics Competition, mentorship programs, and the eStudyUniverse app with AI tutoring, offline practice, and 1v1 challenges.

Whether you are preparing for GCE O Level, A Level, polytechnic concours, or medical entrance exams, eStudyUniverse connects web resources, mobile study tools, and a nationwide student community in one trusted place.

## eStudyUniverse App — Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about downloading the app, GCE and concours coverage, pricing, and offline study.

What is the eStudyUniverse app?

eStudyUniverse is Cameroon’s student companion platform for GCE and concours preparation. The mobile app gives you past questions, an AI tutor, video lessons, offline downloads, XP challenges, and leaderboards in one place.

How do I download eStudyUniverse?

Get the app free on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.estudy.universe) or the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/estudyuniverse/id6760349352), then create your free account to start practicing.

Is the eStudyUniverse app free?

Yes. Core features are free, including past questions, practice, and community learning. Premium features are available for advanced study.

Does the app cover GCE O Level and A Level?

Yes. eStudyUniverse covers Cameroon GCE O Level and A Level subjects with past papers, revision notes, and practice tests. Browse our [O Level hub](https://estudyuniverse.com/gce-o-level/) and [A Level hub](https://estudyuniverse.com/gce-a-level/) on the web.

Do you have concours past questions?

Yes. The app and website include concours preparation for engineering, medical, and teaching entrance exams. See our [Cameroon concours hub](https://estudyuniverse.com/cameroon-concours/).

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Download selected past papers and lessons to study without internet — ideal for revision on the go.

Who can use eStudyUniverse?

Secondary school students, high school learners, pre-university candidates, parents, and teachers preparing for GCE or concours exams in Cameroon.
