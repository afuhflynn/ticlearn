# GPT-6 and other models in ChatGPT

Learn how to choose a model and thinking level in ChatGPT, use Intelligent UI, and understand availability and usage limits.

Updated: yesterday

GPT-6 is rolling out in Chat to eligible Pro, Plus, Enterprise, and Business users. If you don’t see **GPT-6** in the model menu, it may not be available for your account yet.

GPT‑6 Pro, powered by GPT‑6 Astra, is available in ChatGPT for Pro $100, Pro $200, Business and Enterprise plans. Enterprise access also depends on your workspace’s model-access permissions. Plus plans include GPT‑6 Astra in ChatGPT Work and Codex.

Astra requires Codex CLI version **0.153.0 or newer**. If you do not see Astra in Chat, Work, or Codex in the desktop app, open **Menu > Check for Updates** and install the latest available version. Check manually even if you recently updated through an automatic update notification or the update icon in the sidebar.

# Overview

**GPT-6** in Chat lets you choose how much ChatGPT thinks. With Intelligent UI, answers can include text, visuals, and interactive elements. Available models and thinking levels depend on your plan and workspace settings.

## Other models in ChatGPT

GPT-5.6 Sol is designed for complex work across coding, knowledge work and research, cybersecurity, science, computer use, and design. GPT-5.6 Sol Pro is the highest-capability GPT-5.6 option for difficult tasks and longer-running workflows.

GPT-5.6 Luna is becoming the default model for Free and Go users. **GPT-5.6 Sol** Pro remains the Pro option in the GPT-5.6 family. The **GPT-6** rollout does not retire older selectable models.

# Choose a thinking level

In the model menu, **GPT-6** replaces **Latest**. Existing **Latest** selections move to **GPT-6** at the same effort; explicit **GPT-5.6 Sol** selections stay on GPT-5.6. Use the **Thinking** slider to choose your effort. When **GPT-6** is selected, **GPT-6 Sol** powers **Instant**, **Medium**, **High**, and **Extra High**; **Pro** uses GPT-6 Pro, powered by **GPT-6 Astra**. Available options depend on your plan and workspace settings.

- **Instant** — Fast responses for everyday questions. Free and Go use GPT-5.6 Luna.

- **Medium** — Standard thinking.

- **High** — Extended thinking.

- **Extra High** — The highest thinking level available with GPT-6 Sol.

- **Pro** — Pro models for difficult tasks and longer-running workflows. Available models include GPT-5.6 Sol Pro and, on eligible plans, GPT-6 Pro.


On Plus and Pro plans, **Instant** doesn’t automatically switch to a higher thinking level for complex requests. Asking ChatGPT to “think harder” or “think deeply” won’t automatically switch to a different thinking level. ChatGPT can still switch automatically for safety.

Free and Go users can use **Think** for harder questions. Think uses GPT-5.6 Luna, not GPT-5.6 Sol.

Enterprise customers may continue to see the existing model picker until the new controls reach their workspace.

For automatic switching to **Thinking** in managed workspaces, see: [ChatGPT Business models and limits](https://help.openai.com/articles/12003714) and [ChatGPT Enterprise and Edu models and limits](https://help.openai.com/articles/11165333).

# Intelligent UI

With GPT-6 in Chat, Intelligent UI can combine text, visuals, and interactive elements when useful. Ordinary text answers are still possible, and no special prompt is required. A response may begin while ChatGPT continues thinking, followed by more detail.

These capabilities are rolling out on the web and supported updated apps when you use GPT-6 at **Instant** through **Extra High**. They are not available with **Pro** effort, which remains powered by GPT-6 Astra. Availability depends on your plan and workspace access. Update your mobile app to the latest available version. The older ChatGPT desktop apps for macOS and Windows do not support these capabilities; use ChatGPT on the web instead.

Intelligent UI has no separate usage quota. Existing model, tool, and plan allowances still apply. Changes to a conversation’s appearance don’t change your other chats.

## Reduce visual elements

On ChatGPT on the web, open **Settings**. Turn off **Layout and visuals** to reduce visual elements. Some visual elements may still appear; this setting does not switch the entire answer experience to a classic or text-only mode.

# Usage limits

Usage limits depend on your plan, the model and, for managed workspaces, workspace settings. The sections below explain **Thinking** limits and how GPT-6 Pro and **GPT-5.6 Sol** Pro allowances work together.

## Instant limits

Free and Go users have unlimited everyday text chats, subject to abuse-prevention safeguards. Separate limits continue to apply to file uploads, image generation, voice, data analysis, and other tools.

## Thinking usage and fallbacks

Manually choosing **Medium**, **High**, or **Extra High** uses **GPT-6 Sol** when **GPT-6** is selected, or **GPT-5.6 Sol** when you explicitly select GPT-5.6. If you reach a **Thinking** limit, ChatGPT may continue with another available model.

If you are on a Go plan, select **Think** for harder questions. Think uses GPT-5.6 Luna, not **GPT-5.6 Sol**.

ChatGPT displays when an allowance resets when that information is available. You can use another available model or wait until the displayed reset time.

## GPT-6 Pro and GPT-5.6 Sol Pro limits

GPT-6 Pro has usage limits. A Pro subscription does not include unlimited use of the GPT-6 Pro model. ChatGPT Work and Codex have separate allowances.

Pro plans with higher usage allowances include higher limits for Pro models.

On Pro $200, when you reach your GPT-6 Pro weekly limit, ChatGPT automatically switches to GPT-5.6 **Thinking** at **Medium**. To use **GPT-5.6 Sol** Pro, select **GPT-5.6 Sol** in the model menu, then choose Pro. This remains available only while your **GPT-5.6 Sol** Pro daily allowance and combined daily allowance have usage remaining.

On Pro $100, both models draw from one weekly allowance. Switching between them does not provide more messages once that shared allowance is exhausted. Business Standard and Premium also share their included allowance across the two models; switching models does not reset or increase that included allowance.

On a Pro plan, reaching a Pro-model limit does not change your subscription. You can use another available model or wait for the relevant allowance to reset. ChatGPT displays the reset time when that information is available.

## Managed workspaces and the ChatGPT Pro plan

For current managed-workspace limits and credit rates, see: [ChatGPT Business - Models and Limits](https://help.openai.com/articles/12003714), [ChatGPT Enterprise and Edu models and limits](https://help.openai.com/articles/11165333), and the [ChatGPT Rate Card](https://help.openai.com/articles/11481834).

On the ChatGPT Pro plan, some models have separate usage allowances. When you reach a model's allowance, that model may be temporarily unavailable until the allowance resets. Reaching an allowance does not by itself mean that your account was restricted.

For more information, see: [About ChatGPT Pro tiers](https://help.openai.com/articles/9793128).

Usage must follow the [Terms of Use](https://openai.com/policies/terms-of-use/). OpenAI uses safeguards to help prevent misuse, which may occasionally result in a temporary usage restriction. ChatGPT notifies you if this happens.

# GPT-5.6 safeguards

GPT-5.6 includes protections trained into the model, along with real-time checks and monitoring. Some higher-risk biological and cybersecurity requests may be refused or require additional checks. These safeguards can occasionally affect legitimate requests.

# FAQ

## Where is the Higher intelligence setting?

On Plus and Pro plans, the **Higher intelligence** setting isn’t available on ChatGPT web. Use the model picker to choose how much ChatGPT thinks.

In ChatGPT Business, you can find **Higher intelligence** under **Settings > General**. This setting controls whether ChatGPT automatically uses additional thinking while **Instant** is selected.

For more information about automatic thinking in Business, see: [ChatGPT Business models and limits](https://help.openai.com/articles/12003714).

## Why don’t I see GPT-6?

GPT-6 is rolling out to eligible paid accounts. Look for **GPT-6** in the model menu; it is not labeled Sol. If it is missing, it may not be available for your account yet. In a managed workspace, your model-access permissions also apply.

## Why do I not see GPT-5.6 Sol?

**GPT-5.6 Sol** remains a separate option in the model menu. Selecting it keeps your conversation on GPT-5.6 and its available thinking levels.

Confirm that GPT-5.6 Sol is included with your plan and that you are signed in to the correct account. If you use a managed workspace, ask an admin whether the model is available for your workspace or role.

Free and Go plans do not include GPT-5.6 Sol. These plans receive GPT-5.6 Luna.

## What happens to my existing model selection?

**GPT-6** is rolling out as the current default in Chat for eligible Pro, Plus, Enterprise, and Business users. Existing **Latest** selections move to **GPT-6** at the same effort. Explicit **GPT-5.6 Sol** selections stay on GPT-5.6, and older selectable models are not being retired by this rollout. Free and Go users receive GPT-5.6 Luna.

**GPT-6 Sol** powers **Instant** through **Extra High** where available. Pro effort remains GPT-6 Pro, powered by **GPT-6 Astra**; **GPT-5.6 Sol** Pro remains available within the GPT-5.6 family.

## Can I select GPT-5.6 Terra or GPT-5.6 Luna in ChatGPT?

Terra and Luna are not selectable in standard ChatGPT conversations. Depending on your plan, they are available in Work and Codex. Developers can also use them through the OpenAI API.

## Does GPT-5.6 change my ChatGPT usage limits?

Free and Go users have unlimited everyday text chats. File uploads, image generation, voice, data analysis, and other tools remain subject to separate limits.

**Thinking** limits depend on your plan. For managed workspaces, see the plan-specific Models and Limits article or the [ChatGPT Rate Card](https://help.openai.com/articles/11481834) for current limits and credit rates. GPT-6 Pro and GPT-5.6 Sol Pro have the separate or shared Pro-model allowances described above.

## Can Support reset my usage limits?

No. OpenAI Support does not reset ChatGPT or Codex usage limits. If you reach a limit, wait until it resets or use another available option shown in your account.

If you believe your usage was counted incorrectly, or access has not returned after the stated reset time, contact Support so we can investigate.

## Don’t see Astra in the desktop app?

In the ChatGPT desktop app, open **Menu > Check for Updates** and install the latest available version. Check manually even if you recently updated through an automatic update notification or the update icon in the sidebar.

Astra availability in Chat, Work, and Codex depends on your plan and workspace permissions.

For the Codex CLI, Astra requires **version 0.153.0 or later**.
